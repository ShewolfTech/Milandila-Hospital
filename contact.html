Get in touch

How can we help?

Send us a message and tell us what you need. A member of our team can get back to you with the information or assistance you are looking for.

Finding us

We want getting here to feel simple.

When you are coming to hospital, the last thing you need is uncertainty about where to go.

If you are planning a visit and need help finding the hospital, please contact our team before you travel. We can provide the information you need to help you reach us.

Need directions?

Call us on 0793 193 377 or 0793 193 378 and our team will assist you with location information.
