Patient information

We want your visit to feel a little easier.

Knowing what to expect can make a difference. Here you can find helpful information to guide you before, during and after your visit to Milandila Women & Babies Hospital.

01

Before your visit

Give yourself enough time to prepare for your visit. If you are coming for a scheduled service, bring any information or documents you have been asked to provide.

02

During your visit

Our team is here to help you find the right service and understand the care you are receiving. If you are unsure where to go, please speak with a member of the hospital team.

03

After your visit

If you have questions about your care, need further assistance or are unsure about your next step, please contact the hospital team for guidance.
