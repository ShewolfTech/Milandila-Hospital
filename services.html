Our services

Care for women, babies, children and families.

From pregnancy and childbirth to newborn care, children's health and women's healthcare, Milandila Women & Babies Hospital brings essential services together in one place with compassion at the heart of every patient experience.

What we offer

Healthcare for the moments that matter.

Our services are designed to support patients through different stages of their healthcare journey, from routine visits and consultations to maternity care, treatment and emergency support.

01

Skilled Delivery

Bringing a baby into the world is an important and deeply personal experience. Our maternity care supports mothers through labour and delivery with professional attention and compassionate support.

02

Antenatal Care Services

Pregnancy comes with many questions and changes. Antenatal care provides mothers with regular healthcare and support throughout pregnancy, helping them stay informed and prepared for delivery.

03

OB & Gyn Services

We provide care focused on women's reproductive health, including obstetric and gynaecological services for women at different stages of life.

04

Major & Minor Surgeries

When surgery is needed, patients deserve professional care and clear communication. Our surgical services include care for major and minor procedures.

05

Ultrasound Scan

Ultrasound scanning helps healthcare professionals assess and monitor different conditions. Our service supports clinical care with diagnostic imaging when it is needed.

06

Laboratory Services

Laboratory testing plays an important role in understanding a patient's health. Our laboratory services support healthcare teams with information needed for diagnosis and patient care.

07

Neonatal Care Services

Newborn babies sometimes need extra attention and support after birth. Our neonatal services provide care for babies who require additional monitoring or specialised support.

08

Family Planning

Family planning gives individuals and couples the opportunity to make informed decisions about their reproductive health. Our service provides a supportive setting for family planning care and guidance.

09

Immunisations

Immunisation is an important part of protecting children and families from preventable illnesses. We provide immunisation services to support healthy growth and development.

10

Counselling

Sometimes good healthcare means having someone who will listen. Our counselling service provides a supportive space for people who need guidance, emotional support or someone to talk to.

11

24/7 Emergency Care

Medical emergencies can happen at any time. Our emergency service is available around the clock so that patients can seek urgent medical attention when they need it.

12

EMCT Services

Our EMCT service forms part of the hospital's wider healthcare support. For information about this service and the care available, please contact our team.

13

Outpatient Pediatrics

Children need healthcare that recognises their unique needs. Our outpatient paediatric services provide care for children without the need for an overnight hospital stay.

14

Lactation Consultation

Breastfeeding can take time, patience and support. Our lactation consultation service helps mothers and babies receive guidance and support as they navigate breastfeeding.

Care that continues

You do not have to figure everything out on your own.

Healthcare can sometimes feel confusing, especially when you are worried about yourself or someone you love.

Our team is here to help you understand the care you need and guide you towards the appropriate service. If you are unsure where to begin, contact us and we will help you take the next step.

Looking for a specific service?

Speak with our team about your healthcare needs and we can help you find the appropriate point of care.

Our approach

Professional care with a personal touch.

We believe patients deserve more than a clinical service. They deserve to be listened to, treated with dignity and given the opportunity to understand what is happening with their health.

Whether you are visiting us for a routine appointment or seeking urgent care, our goal is to make your experience as clear, respectful and reassuring as possible.

Need care?

We are here when you need us.

If you have questions about our services or need help deciding where to begin, get in touch with our team. For emergencies, our emergency service is available 24/7.
