About Milandila

Healthcare with a human heart.

At Milandila Women & Babies Hospital, we believe that good healthcare is about more than treating an illness. It is about listening to people, understanding what they are going through and making sure they feel cared for throughout their journey.

Who we are

A place where women, babies and families come first.

Milandila Women & Babies Hospital is committed to providing quality healthcare in an environment where patients feel safe, respected and understood.

From pregnancy and childbirth to newborn care, paediatric services and women's health, our work is centred around the needs of individuals and families. We understand that every patient arrives with a different story, different concerns and different needs.

That is why we believe in care that is personal. We take the time to listen, explain and support our patients so they can feel confident about the care they receive.

Care that puts people first Every patient deserves to be treated with kindness, dignity and respect.

What we believe

Excellence means caring about the person, not just the condition.

Our approach to healthcare is grounded in compassion, professionalism and respect for every person who comes through our doors.

♡

Compassionate care

We know that being unwell or seeking medical care can be an emotional experience. We strive to make every patient feel welcomed, listened to and treated with kindness.

✚

Quality healthcare

We are committed to providing professional healthcare that puts the wellbeing of our patients at the centre of every decision and every interaction.

◌

Respect for every family

Every family is different. We listen to individual needs, respect personal choices and work with patients and their families throughout their care.

Our promise

You should never feel like just another patient.

A hospital visit can be frightening, especially when you are worried about yourself or someone you love. We believe that a reassuring word, a patient explanation and a listening ear can make a meaningful difference.

Our promise is to approach every patient with empathy and professionalism. We want you to understand your care, feel comfortable asking questions and know that your wellbeing matters to us.

Our patients

Caring for the whole family.

Our focus on women, babies and children allows us to support families through some of the most important moments of their lives.

Whether you are visiting us for an antenatal appointment, preparing for delivery, seeking care for your newborn, bringing your child for treatment or looking after your own health, we are here to walk alongside you.

We want Milandila to be a place where families feel comfortable returning when they need care and confident recommending us to the people they love.

Our values

The kind of care we want you to remember.

Healthcare is personal. These values guide how we want every patient and family to experience Milandila.

01

Empathy

We listen before we respond. We seek to understand what our patients are experiencing and meet them with patience and compassion.

02

Dignity

Every person deserves to be treated with respect, regardless of their circumstances, background or reason for seeking care.

03

Excellence

We continually strive to provide dependable, professional care and an experience that gives patients confidence in their healthcare journey.

What guides us

Excellence Rooted In Empathy

These words capture what we believe healthcare should feel like. Excellence gives our patients confidence in the care they receive. Empathy reminds us that behind every appointment, every procedure and every diagnosis is a person who deserves to be heard and cared for.

At Milandila, we bring these two ideas together because excellent healthcare should never lose its human side.

We are here for you

Your health matters to us.

Whether you are planning a visit, preparing for motherhood, looking for care for your child or simply need to speak with our team, we are ready to help.
